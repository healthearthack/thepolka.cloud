Brewery OT/ICS Cyber Security & NIST SP 800-82 Playbook

Modern craft brewhouses rely on automated PLCs, Modbus/TCP networks, and SCADA telemetry to control mash temperatures, glycol jackets, and CIP cycles. A cyber compromise can spoil thousands of gallons or cause physical tank ruptures. Here is how NIST frameworks secure the brewery.

1. Purdue Model Segmentation (NIST SP 800-82 §5.2)

Strictly air-gap Level 0/1 field instrumentation (kettle sensors, VFD pumps) and Level 2 PLCs from Level 4/5 enterprise networks. No guest taproom Wi-Fi may ever route to Modbus port 502.

2. NIST CSF 2.0: IDENTIFY (ID.AM)

Maintain an immutable inventory of all fermentation controllers, valve actuators, and SCADA HMIs. Identify cryptographic hashes of authorized brewing profiles before execution.

3. NIST CSF 2.0: PROTECT (PR.AC & PR.DS)

Implement Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) for industrial protocols. Enforce role-based access control and cryptographically sign recipe files with SHA-256 HMACs to prevent setpoint tampering.

4. NIST CSF 2.0: DETECT (DE.CM)

Continuous SCADA anomaly detection. Monitor temperature drift rates, pressure fluctuations, and unexpected Modbus write commands (Function Codes 06 & 16) in real-time.

5. NIST CSF 2.0: RESPOND (RS.RP)

Physical emergency stops and isolated fail-safe valves override software commands. If SCADA telemetry drifts beyond safe brewing margins, automated relays isolate heating loops.

6. NIST CSF 2.0: RECOVER (RC.RP & NIST SP 800-193)

Platform Firmware Resiliency: Maintain immutable offline backups of PLC ladder logic. Restore verified baseline configurations in minutes without paying ransomware or dumping wort.

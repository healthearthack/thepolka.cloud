Concept campaign

G.M.A.I.L. = Google My Artificial Intelligence Language

Email can contain rich personal language data. The proposal: if a user affirmatively elects to contribute derived language signals for Artificial Intelligence research, participation should be transparent, revocable, scoped, and potentially rewarded.

Plugin 01 · Draft Agent

Read permitted inbound email, prepare a response, save it to Drafts, and require a human to review and press Send.

Plugin 02 · Data Contribution Consent

Proposed opt-in controls for categories, purpose, duration, exclusions, and one-click withdrawal.

Proposal · Credit for contribution

Campaign concept: users who deliberately contribute eligible data could receive Google Artificial Intelligence credits or other benefits. This is not a current Google program.

Guardrails

Exclude passwords, security codes, financial identifiers, sensitive records, private attachments, and third-party correspondence without an appropriate consent basis.

Google states that Workspace data is not used to train underlying generative Artificial Intelligence models without permission. This site proposes an explicit compensated opt-in model; it does not claim Google currently pays users for Gmail training data.
